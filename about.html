Mission

Our mission & values

We prioritize security, simplicity, and speed. Our goal is to provide teams with tools that reduce friction and increase delivery velocity.

Security-first

AES-256 encryption, role-based access, and detailed audit logs.

User-first UX

Intuitive interfaces and fast workflows to keep teams focused.

Transparent pricing

No per-seat surprises — predictable plans with full feature access.

Product

Product highlights

Built for teams that need structure and security. Key features include authentication & user management, flexible project boards, task collaboration, real-time notifications, and email integrations.

Authentication & User Management

JWT-based auth, role-based access control, and profile management to keep teams organized and secure.

Project & Task Management

Kanban-style boards, drag-and-drop, assignments, priorities, and due dates for predictable delivery.

Real-time Notifications & Email

WebSocket-powered updates plus email notifications for invites, assignments, and critical alerts.

Our Journey

From frustration to innovation

We built TeamBoard because we were tired of choosing between simple but underpowered tools and complex enterprise bloatware.

2019

The Beginning

Founded by three frustrated engineers in a garage. We launched the first prototype focused purely on speed and Kanban basics.

2021

Going Real-Time

Remote work exploded. We pivoted to real-time collaboration, introducing WebSocket-powered live updates that changed the game.

2023

Security First

Enterprises came knocking. We launched the Credentials Vault and advanced RBAC, becoming the secure choice for agencies and production teams.
