Seamless Real-Time Collaboration

Break down silos and work together as one. TeamBoard's real-time engine ensures everyone stays on the same page, instantly. No more refreshing to see changes.

  • Instant Sync Updates happen instantly via WebSockets. See cards move and comments appear in real-time.
  • Presence Indicators Know exactly who is viewing or editing a task with live presence avatars.
  • Threaded Comments Have focused discussions directly on tasks with support for @mentions and emojis.
